Battery Guardian (Tablet Host): Protects the tablet's battery health. When battery drops below 25%, it sends a push notification to your phone via ntfy.sh and sends an MQTT alert. When it reaches 80%, it notifies you to unplug it.
PC Agent (Windows 11): Runs silently on your PC. Sends a heartbeat every 15s and checks GPU temp (nvidia-smi). If GPU goes above 80°C, it alerts the tablet.
Automation Relay (Debian PRoot): A lightweight Python script (<30 MB RAM) that connects the dots:
PC shuts down → Lights fade to night/idle mode.
PC turns on → Lights turn on work mode.
GPU overheats → Desk backlights pulse red.
Tablet battery is low → Room bulb turns amber as a visual warning.
Smart Light Bridge: Calls your Alexa/Tuya smart devices without heavy software like Home Assistant.
